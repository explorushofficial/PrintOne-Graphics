name: PrintOne quote flow
description: Product and enquiry model for the PrintOne Graphics website.
---

PrintOne's catalogue represents customised design, print, publication, finishing and gifting work, not fixed-price retail stock. Do not invent product prices or represent enquiry selection as a completed purchase.

**Why:** The supplied business content describes services but includes no fixed catalogue prices or checkout terms; quantities and specifications need discussion.

**How to apply:** Keep service selections as an enquiry list that opens WhatsApp with the chosen work and quantity. Add pricing or payment only if the owner supplies the catalogue and requests those functions.
